How to use

Writing

  1. Tap the line you want to write on, or drag the paper until that line sits in the input slot.
  2. Type. The date is stamped for you.
  3. Done writing? Tap an empty part of the paper.
  4. The button at the bottom cycles through Write, Read and Locked.

When you only want to read, switch to Locked. In Locked mode, tapping a line won't open the input.

Notebooks

Keep separate notebooks: daily, goals, ideas.

  1. Tap the notebook icon at the bottom to open the notebooks panel.
  2. Tap a notebook twice to switch to it.
  3. A new notebook opens as soon as you give it a name.

Search

Search the notebook you're in.

  1. Tap the magnifier icon at the bottom and type a word.
  2. Every line with that word gathers, each with its date.
  3. Tap a line to go to that day, that line.
  4. Or pick a date from the “Past days” list to jump there.

Font and paper

Pick the font, paper tone and ruling you like.

  1. Tap the settings icon at the bottom. The settings panel opens.
  2. Choose the font and text size, the paper tone and the ruling.
  3. The paper changes as you choose.
  4. Haptics turn on and off with the haptics icon at the bottom.
